EducationalKidsVideoMaker ("the app") is a personal, single-operator automation project. It is not a public product, has no public sign-up, and no third party ever creates an account with it or authorizes it against their own data. This page exists because platform developer programs (YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API, Meta's Instagram API) require a Terms of Service URL even for a personal-use tool like this one.

What this app does

The app generates original, pre-produced children's educational video episodes (phonics, counting, colors and shapes, animals, basic science, emotions/manners) for the "Paula the Penguin Learns" YouTube channel, and publishes them — after mandatory human review — to accounts its own operator owns: that YouTube channel, and a TikTok and Instagram account for the same channel. It does not offer any service to the public and does not accept authorization from any account other than the operator's own.
