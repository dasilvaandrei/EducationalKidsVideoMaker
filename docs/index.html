Kids Educational Content

also known internally as EducationalKidsVideoMaker

Kids Educational Content is an automated content-production pipeline that generates original educational video episodes for children under 8 — covering phonics, counting, colors and shapes, animals, basic science, and emotions/manners — and publishes them to a single YouTube channel owned by the developer.

Every episode is scripted, voiced, and rendered by the pipeline, then reviewed by a human before it is ever published. Every upload is declared Made for Kids.

What this app is (and isn't)

This is not a consumer product and has no public sign-up. It is a private automation tool that uses the YouTube Data API's youtube.upload scope to publish finished video files to the developer's own channel on a schedule.
